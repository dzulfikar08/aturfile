# aturfile privacy policy

*Last updated 21 November 2026*

aturfile does not collect, store or send any personal data.

- **No network.** The app makes no network connections of its own. It has no analytics, no crash reporting, no ads and no accounts.
- **Your files stay on your Mac.** aturfile reads folders only to show them to you, search them and measure their size. Nothing about your files leaves your Mac.
- **Folder access.** In the Mac App Store version, macOS asks you which folders aturfile may open. aturfile remembers those choices as bookmarks inside its own container on your Mac, and you can remove them at any time.
- **Settings** (theme, view, recent folders) are saved in a file on your Mac and never shared.
- **Purchases.** Buying aturfile Pro is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. aturfile learns only whether Pro is unlocked; it never sees your payment details or Apple Account.

If this ever changes, this page will say so before the app does.

Questions: open an issue at [github.com/dzulfikar08/aturfile/issues](https://github.com/dzulfikar08/aturfile/issues).
