# aturfile support

**Report a problem or ask a question:** [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/dzulfikar08/aturfile/issues/new). Include your macOS version and what you were doing. Replies usually come within two working days.

## Common questions

**aturfile can't open a folder.** The Mac App Store version can only open folders you have granted. Click *Choose Folder…* in the folder and pick it in the panel; choosing your Home folder once covers everything in it. aturfile remembers the choice.

**How do I restore my Pro purchase?** Press ⌘K and choose *Restore Purchases*, or open the Pro sheet and click *Restore*.

**Is Clean Up safe?** Clean Up only suggests caches and build leftovers that apps recreate on their own, never documents. Items go to the Trash, not straight to deletion, so you can put them back.

**What does Pro include?** More than one tab, and Move to Trash for the Clean Up queue. Everything else is free.
